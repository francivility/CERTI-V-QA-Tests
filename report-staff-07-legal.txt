8. HYPOTHESIS UNDER TEST (from the test brief)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Database row contention and transaction failures on calendar models when
  multiple legal officers attempt to compute and write to overlapping schedule
  slots at the same instant.

Status: NOT OBSERVED and NOT EXERCISED. The main case is handled in the code;
        two narrower gaps remain.

Evidence from the backend source:
- Handled: HearingSlot has a unique constraint on (date, time)
  (legal/models.py:22), and schedule_hearing books inside transaction.atomic
  with select_for_update on the slot row (legal/views.py:301-306). Two
  officers booking the same slot are serialized; the second receives 400 "This
  time slot is already booked". That is brief, intended row contention, not a
  failure.

- Gap: the daily limit is checked outside the lock (day_booking_count,
  legal/views.py:297; MAX_SLOTS_PER_DAY = 4 in legal/utils.py). Concurrent
  bookings of different slots on one day can exceed it.

- Gap: the only exception handled around the booking is Hearing.DoesNotExist
  (views.py:318). Two simultaneous requests for the same application on
  different slots would violate the one-hearing-per-application constraint and
  surface as an unhandled 500.

- Heavier than scheduling: send_notice, preview-notice, preview-decision and
  forward-to-ctdo each build a DOCX and convert it to PDF through Microsoft
  Word on the request thread (legal/views.py:628, :730, :977, :1053).

- Spec corrections: check-availability is a GET with ?date and ?time (the
  brief lists POST); preview-notice and preview-decision are POST (the brief
  lists GET); /sign/<token>/upload/ is mounted at the site root, not under
  /api.

- To test the gaps: run with -e MUTATE=1 against disposable applications in
  the legal queue. The script already makes every VU book slots on the same
  day.
