8. HYPOTHESIS UNDER TEST (from the test brief)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Severe CPU bottlenecking within the backend thread execution engine due to
  processing heavy cryptographic digital certificate signing algorithms
  directly on the synchronous response path.

Status: NOT OBSERVED and NOT EXERCISED. Contradicted by the code.

Evidence from the backend source:
- Contradicted: nothing in the backend signs with a certificate.
  requirements.txt has no signing library (cryptography, pyHanko, endesive and
  pyOpenSSL were searched for) and the code has no x509 or PKCS usage.

- What "attach signature" does: after a password check it pastes the officer's
  stored PNG into the Decision DOCX and converts the result to PDF
  (legal/views.py:1188-1189 for CTDO, :1296-1297 for ORD). approve_decision
  rebuilds and converts the document again (:1332).

- Consistent in part: that work is synchronous on the request thread, and the
  conversion goes through Microsoft Word (legal/document_generation.py:555),
  which is slow and effectively serial. Concurrent approvals would queue
  behind it. The bottleneck would be Word, not a signing algorithm.

- Worth stating plainly in the project record: the e-signature is an image
  stamp gated by a password, not a digital signature. The PDF carries no
  cryptographic proof of who signed it.

- These four views are the ones whose role restriction is correctly applied
  (legal/views.py:1602-1605).
